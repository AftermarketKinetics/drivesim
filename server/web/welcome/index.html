drivesim

Practise driving your combat robot before it exists: upload its CAD, answer a few questions, and drive it in NHRL's 16 ft cage, from the driver's box, with your own radio. Physics include the drive motors, tyre grip and your weapon's gyroscopic effect.

drivesim is invite-only and uses your combat-robot.systems account. No account yet? See wiki.combat-robot.systems for how to get one.
