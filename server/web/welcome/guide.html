How it works
  1. Export the whole robot as a STEP assembly from your CAD (not one part), with part names kept. Fasteners and electronics can stay in.
  2. Upload it below. A big assembly takes a minute or two to import.
  3. Answer Claude's questions. From the CAD alone it can't tell which parts are wheels and weapon, which way the weapon spins, what things are made of, or which motors and gear ratios you use. It asks in one message; answer what you know, and it writes and checks the robot file.
  4. When the page says "Ready to drive", click "Drive it". The sim runs in Chrome or Edge (it needs WebGPU). Your robot starts facing an orange copy of itself, which sits armed with its sticks centred. The "Opponent" button (top right) swaps it for an orange box as heavy as your robot, and back; each switch restarts the round.
  5. Plug in your radio (below) and move a stick so the browser sees it. Arm with SA.

The top-left readout shows the radio, arming, speed, weapon rpm, tilt and "physics N× real time": below 1× the sim can't keep up and runs slow.

Set up your radio (EdgeTX + ELRS)

Download accelerate-elrs-pocket.yml: a model for the RadioMaster Pocket on EdgeTX 2.10. The same sticks and switches drive the sim and a real ELRS robot, so what you practise is what you drive.

ChannelControlDoes
CH1right stick left/rightturn
CH2right stick up/downdrive
CH3left stick up/downweapon (stick down = off)
CH5SAarm (down = armed)

CH4 (left stick left/right) and CH6–8 (SD, SE, SB) are spare: they do nothing in the sim, and are there for robots that use them.

  1. Copy the model onto the radio. Plug the radio in by USB and choose USB Storage (or put its SD card in a reader). Open MODELS/ and copy the file in as modelNN.yml, using a number that isn't there yet: copying onto an existing number replaces that model.
  2. Select it: eject, press MDL and pick Accelerate. Zero any trims. On load, the radio wants SA up and the weapon stick down.
  3. For the sim: plug in by USB and choose USB Joystick (HID). Leave the joystick mode on Classic, the default. Open the drive page and move a stick; the readout changes from "NO RADIO" to "radio connected".
  4. For a real robot: the Pocket's internal ELRS module sends the same channels. Bind your receiver as usual; ELRS arms from CH5.

Another EdgeTX radio works too: give it a model with the four channels above (CH1 aileron, CH2 elevator, CH3 throttle, CH5 a two-position switch), no expo or rates, and Classic USB joystick mode.
